# mAIcroverse

mAIcro simplifies and accelerates how you and AI build prototypes and personal software together. 

mAIcro provides the tools required to build and work with databases, documents, files and day to dayprocesses, so you can turn an idea into something that enables people to explore and provide feedback.

Prototypes make new ideas easier to share. Showing a working experience is more powerful than describing one: people can see what you mean, have many questions answered immediately, and ask better questions about what to explore next.

mAIcroverse is the friendly place to discover this way of working. It gives you an isolated learning environment with guided courses, reusable examples, and clean starting points you can reset whenever you want to try again. Your experiments stay separate from the projects you are actively building.

## Start Here
